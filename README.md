# 🏠🎉 US Real Estate Sales Analysis (2001–2021) 📈

Analyze Connecticut residential and commercial property sales from 2001 to 2021 to uncover price growth, town rankings, and distribution by property type. 🚀

## 📝 Article

You can access the full article here: [Report](https://anpabelt.medium.com/analyzing-two-decades-of-u-s-real-estate-sales-a-python-visualization-approach-de23e7fb0479) 📝

## 📊 Overview 😎

This project analyzes Connecticut real estate transactions over 20 years:

1. 🏷️ Median sale-price growth
2. 🗺️ Town-by-town average price comparison
3. 🏘️ Property-type distribution

## 🗂️ Data 📦

- **CSV:** `Real_Estate_Sales_2001-2021_GL 2.csv` 📑
- **Notebook:** `Real Estate Sales 2001 - 2021 in the US.ipynb` 📓
